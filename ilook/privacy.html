iLook 隐私政策

适用范围

iLook 是用于浏览和播放用户自行选择的视频的应用，无需创建 iLook 账号。可选功能连接用户选择的服务器或第三方服务，它们有各自的隐私政策和数据保留规则。

设备上的信息

  • 影片库可能包含用户选择的文件名、路径、影片资料和封面，以及收藏、播放进度、播放列表和设置。iLook 在设备上保存这些信息，以提供浏览、播放和续播功能。下载、导入或通过局域网传输的视频保存在设备上；封面等可能被缓存以便再次使用。
  • 添加 SMB、WebDAV、FTP、NFS 或 Jellyfin 等来源时，应用使用用户提供的地址和所需账号信息连接该来源。连接密码及 Jellyfin 会话令牌保存在设备的钥匙串中。服务器和所选协议可能接收认证信息、文件路径、检索词、设备标识和其他完成请求所需的信息。用户对 Jellyfin 内容进行收藏或标记观看状态时，相应操作会发送给该服务器。服务器是否加密连接取决于所选协议和服务器配置；普通 HTTP 和 FTP 不提供传输加密。
  • 用户可授权本地网络访问，以发现或连接局域网服务，并可主动启用浏览器或 FTP 传输接收文件；传输会在用户停止、离开页面或应用进入非活动状态时停止。浏览器传输使用未加密的本地 HTTP，应在可信网络使用。用户也可通过系统文件选择器授权访问文件或文件夹。
  • 用户主动开启 iTunes 同步视频后，iLook 请求媒体资料库权限，读取本机已下载、没有 DRM 保护的视频及相关资料，用于浏览和播放。权限可在系统设置中更改。
  • 本机诊断是可选功能，记录播放、扫描及扫描问题的汇总次数，供用户在应用中查看；应用不会自动上传该摘要。关闭诊断会清除这些计数。

连接第三方服务

  • 用户播放在线演示影片或输入的网络地址时，设备直接连接对应内容服务。服务提供方可能收到 IP 地址、请求的资源及其他正常网络请求信息。部分演示影片由 W3C 或 Internet Archive 提供，分别适用其自身隐私政策。
  • 使用用户选择的 NAS、媒体服务器或文件服务时，相关服务依据其配置处理请求。iLook 不控制这些服务的日志、保留期限或隐私做法。请向该服务的运营者了解详情。
  • 正式版本不启用 TMDB 在线影片匹配功能；影片资料可来自用户的媒体服务器或文件旁的 NFO 和图片。

保留、删除和用户选择

媒体库及设置保存在设备上，直到用户移除相应连接或内容、删除应用数据或由系统清理可清除的缓存。移除连接会删除应用中对应的连接凭据及相关片库记录。用户可在“本机副本”页面删除导入或下载的本机视频副本；删除副本不会删除原始服务器文件。如果用户启用文件管理并明确确认删除来源文件，该操作会影响来源文件。删除应用后，设备备份或系统钥匙串中可能仍保留部分数据，具体取决于系统行为和用户的备份设置。服务器及第三方服务上的数据需按其各自的方式管理或删除。

用户可在系统设置中撤销本地网络或媒体资料库权限，也可在应用内关闭本机诊断或停止局域网传输。撤销权限可能影响依赖相应功能的使用。

iLook Privacy Policy

Scope

iLook is an app for browsing and playing videos you choose. You do not need an iLook account. Optional features connect to servers or third-party services you select, which have their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Information on your device

  • Your library may contain selected file names, paths, video information and artwork, as well as favorites, playback progress, playlists and settings. iLook stores these on your device to provide browsing, playback and resume features. Videos downloaded, imported or transferred over the local network are stored on the device; artwork and other data may be cached for later use.
  • When you add a source such as SMB, WebDAV, FTP, NFS or Jellyfin, the app uses the address and any account information you provide to connect to it. Connection passwords and Jellyfin session tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Depending on the service and protocol, the server may receive credentials, file paths, search terms, a device identifier and other information needed to fulfill requests. If you favorite an item or mark it watched on Jellyfin, that action is sent to the server. Encryption depends on the chosen protocol and server configuration; ordinary HTTP and FTP do not encrypt transfers.
  • You may allow local network access to discover or connect to services and choose to receive files using browser or FTP transfer. Transfer stops when you stop it, leave the corresponding screen or the app becomes inactive. Browser transfer uses unencrypted local HTTP and should be used on a trusted network. You can also grant access to files or folders with the system file picker.
  • If you enable iTunes synced videos, iLook requests media library access and reads locally downloaded, DRM-free videos and related information for browsing and playback. You can change this permission in system settings.
  • Optional on-device diagnostics records aggregate counts of playbacks, scans and scans with issues for you to view in the app. The app does not automatically upload this summary. Turning diagnostics off clears these counts.

Third-party connections

  • When you play an online demo or a network address you enter, your device connects directly to the content service. The provider may receive your IP address, the requested resource and other ordinary network request information. Some demos are hosted by W3C or Internet Archive, each of which has its own privacy policy.
  • NAS devices, media servers and file services you choose process requests according to their own configurations. iLook does not control their logs, retention periods or privacy practices. Contact the relevant service operator for details.
  • The release version does not enable online TMDB title matching. Video information can come from your media server or from NFO and image files alongside your videos.

Retention, deletion and choices

Your library and settings remain on the device until you remove the relevant connection or content, delete app data, or the system clears disposable caches. Removing a connection deletes its stored credential and related library records in the app. You can delete imported or downloaded local video copies on the Local Copies screen; this does not delete the original server file. If you enable file management and explicitly confirm deletion of a source file, that action affects the source file. After you delete the app, some information may remain in device backups or the system Keychain, depending on system behavior and your backup settings. Manage or delete information held by servers and third-party services through those services.

You can revoke local network or media library permissions in system settings, and turn off on-device diagnostics or stop local transfer in the app. Revoking a permission may affect features that depend on it.
