Ginkgo Folio Support

Ginkgo Folio is an iPad reader for your own PDFs: mark them up with Apple Pencil and get AI answers written in the margins. For help, email jiaqianjing@gmail.com with your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send your API keys or the books themselves.
