Ginkgo Folio Privacy Policy

In short

Ginkgo Folio has no server of its own and no accounts. Your books, marks, notes and handwriting stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud, which the developer cannot access. Text goes anywhere else only when you use an AI feature, and then it goes straight to the AI service you chose, using your own API key, after you have agreed.

On your device and in your iCloud

  • The PDFs you import; your highlights, underlines, notes, AI answers and follow-up questions, handwriting, scratch sheets and reading positions; and your settings (tools, colours, AI service and model, answer language, learner level).
  • Your AI service API key, kept in the device Keychain. It is never synced or sent anywhere except to that service.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, everything above except the API key and settings syncs through iCloud (a private CloudKit database and iCloud Drive) to your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data lives in your iCloud account; the developer cannot see it. Without iCloud it stays on this device.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Data in iCloud can be deleted in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

What is sent to an AI service

When you translate, explain, ask, follow up or request a grammar analysis, Ginkgo Folio sends the following directly to the service you chose in Settings (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini), using your own API key:

  • the passage you selected and the paragraphs around it;
  • the book title;
  • your question and earlier follow-ups on that note;
  • your answer-language and learner-level settings.

Before the first request to each service, the app explains this and asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › AI; the app will ask again the next time you use AI. These requests do not pass through any server of the developer, who never sees or stores them. Each service handles what it receives under its own terms:

  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini API: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms (content sent on Gemini's free tier may be used by Google to improve its products)

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Ginkgo Folio 隐私政策

简单说

Ginkgo Folio 没有自己的服务器，也没有账号系统。你的书、划线、批注和笔迹保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 iCloud，还会保存在你自己的 iCloud 里，开发者无法访问。只有在你使用 AI 功能、并且同意之后，相关文字才会用你自己的 API key 直接发给你选的 AI 服务商。

保存在设备和你的 iCloud 里的内容

  • 你导入的 PDF；划线、高亮、批注、AI 回答和追问、手写笔迹、草稿和阅读位置；以及设置（工具、颜色、AI 服务商和模型、回答语言、外语水平）。
  • 你填写的 AI 服务 API key，保存在系统钥匙串里，不同步，除了发给对应的服务商以外不发往任何地方。
  • 登录 iCloud 时，除 API key 和设置以外的内容会通过 iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库和 iCloud 云盘）同步到你登录同一个 Apple 账户的设备。这些数据在你自己的 iCloud 账户里，开发者看不到。没有 iCloud 时只保存在本机。
  • 删除应用会删除本机上的数据。iCloud 里的数据可以在 设置 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理储存空间 中删除。

发给 AI 服务商的内容

你使用翻译、解释、提问、追问或语法分析时，应用会用你自己的 API key，把下面这些内容直接发给你在设置里选的服务商（OpenAI、Anthropic 或 Google Gemini）：

  • 你选中的原文，以及它前后的几段文字；
  • 书名；
  • 你的问题和这条批注之前的追问；
  • 回答语言和外语水平这两项设置。

第一次向某家服务商发送之前，应用会先说明并征得你的同意。你可以随时在「设置 › AI 服务」里关掉，下次使用 AI 时会再问你。这些请求不经过开发者的任何服务器，开发者看不到也不保存。各服务商按各自的条款处理收到的内容（链接见上文英文部分；注意 Gemini 免费额度下提交的内容，Google 可能用于改进其产品）。

不做的事

  • 不做数据分析，不追踪使用情况，没有广告。
  • 不出售你的数据，也不与任何其他人分享。
